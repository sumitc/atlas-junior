🌍 Atlas Junior v1.0.17

This update makes games feel smoother, fairer, and easier to play:

• A reliable turn timer keeps each round moving and helps games finish on time
• Voice input is easier to use, with clearer on-screen guidance while you speak or type
• Better place checking catches invalid answers sooner and gives more helpful feedback
• Missing places can now be requested from inside the game, so new names are easier to add
