🎮 How to play
1
Add two or more players on your phone or tablet.
2
Each turn, name a real place — country, city, river, anything — starting with the given letter.
3
Tap the mic and speak, or just type it in. No duplicate places allowed!
4
The player who names the most places wins. Submit your score to the global leaderboard!

🔧 How it's built

Atlas Junior is free and open source. Here's a plain-English look at the pieces that make it work:

📱 The App
Android (Capacitor)
Built with Next.js & React. Runs entirely on your device — no login needed.
🎙️ Voice Input
On-device speech
Android's built-in speech recognition. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
🏆 Leaderboard
Vercel + Redis
Scores stored in a fast cloud database. Only your chosen team name and score.
🛟 Support
GitHub Issues
Feedback sent in-app goes straight to the developer as a GitHub issue.

💬 Got a question or idea?

Found a bug? Have a feature request? Want a new game mode? We'd love to hear from you — every message is read by a real person.
